// INITIATIVE

An effort to
make open science accessible.

I believe that academic visibility should not be gated by budgets, platforms or technical expertise! Every researcher - regardless of discipline, Institution or location, deserves a digital identity! As one myself, I understand how hard it is to manage everything! So I tried to make it bit easier for us all!

This project uses JSON (a simple text format) to manage your website's content. You can update your site just by typing your information into a text file. While advanced customization can be limited without coding, I am happy to help you adjust the design. Just send me a message. I do this for free, though donations are always welcome to help support and expand this project.
