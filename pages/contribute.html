// Contribute

How You Can
Contribute.

01

Design New Templates

Create new academic website templates that match the AVI aesthetic. Whether it's a CV, lab portal, or project page — your design helps someone present their work better.

02

Write a Blog Post

Share your knowledge; write a guide how to use academic tools, simple thank note about experience of using AVI templates, write about academic visibility, open science, or digital tools for researchers. Your words inspire the community.

03

Donate & Support

Running this project takes time and resources. Your donation helps keep the servers running and allows us to create more free templates. Every contribution matters.

Support on Ko-fi
04

Translate Projects

Help us make AVI accessible to a global audience by translating templates, documentation, and the website into other languages.

05

Share & Promote

Share AVI with your colleagues, department, or university. A simple mention in a mailing list or social media post helps the initiative reach more researchers.

06

Report Issues

Found a broken link, typo, or something that doesn't look right? Let us know. Your feedback helps us improve the site for everyone.

If you are interested in any of the above please send me a message, I'd love to hear from you! Please be sure to check your spam folder, as my replies can sometimes end up there.

Send Us a Message

What's in It for You?

This is a non-profit initiative, so we can't offer financial compensation. But you'll have the joy of contributing to something meaningful, and we'll proudly mention you as one of our contributors under the credits of this project!
