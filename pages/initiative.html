Why I Started
the Initiative?

This small initiative was born out of gratitude. It is my way of giving back to the foundation and who helped me to build that foundation which brought me this far: Education.

This is Dedicated to the teachers, mentors, researchers, and peers who supported my journey and to all the others who's doing the same!

I am trying put into practice some of the technical skills and design principles which I learned as a master's student for last three years. I belive these professional web designs, will help you to have a professional digital footprint while saving some time, money as scholar or a graduate student.

Presentation is
an Art. Just like
your Discovery.

If data is the silent language of discovery, then design is the voice that translates it for the world.

In academia, research is often confined to wordy paper, poster or buried folder. I believe design is an underrated aspect of storytelling that, when combined with the internet, transforms narratives into unforgettable web experiences. which are very powerful in this digital era.


Proper design is essential to guide the visitor's eye. These templates can make sure that your important information and impactful work is never missed. Science is evolving, and so are the internet and our web presence. Therefore, the delivery must evolve too. see it yourself

http://www.university.edu/~smith_lab/index.html

Overall appearance, system fonts signal a "Not active or Under maintenance" status.

Zero to Live in 10 Minutes.

As a researcher, educator, or student, your mental energy and time is your most precious resource. If you don't have the time or if you don't enjoy coding or designing, you shouldn't have to learn them to share your findings.
We use a human-readable text-based format for organizing and storing data called JSON which you do not need programming knowledge to master. The markup features simple, descriptive keys, allowing you to swap out values directly inside a standard text editor, or directly in GitHub, save, and launch instantly.
Check it yourself, click and edit any value on left JSON file to edit!
profile.json
