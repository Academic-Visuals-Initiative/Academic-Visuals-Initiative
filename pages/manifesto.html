Defeating Link Rot.

While building a website often requires technical expertise or professional support; Maintaining a website is difficult once a project ends, budget is tight or when local servers are updated! Older research pages often break or disappear. Academic Visuals Initiative addresses this by building on GitHub's stable, version-controlled hosting, guaranteeing your site stays online with zero ongoing maintenance.

Because your site is a collection of static files and editable JSON, it is portable, archivable, and permanent. Your work stays live as long as the internet exists.

Digital Literacy for Researchers

Simple, actionable guides to mastering modern communicative tools that benefit your career development and elevate your professional standing.

Visual Communication for Academics

Visual clarity is a key part of a strong submission. A clear graphical abstract helps busy reviewers quickly grasp your methodology and results, setting a professional tone for the peer-review process.

Dynamic Presentations & Interactive Storytelling

Dense, text-heavy slides can make it difficult for an audience to follow your presentation. Effective academic communication relies on a clear, visually supported narrative that helps peers and evaluators stay focused on your core findings.

Code for Research

Programming is a powerful tool for any academic. It shouldn't feel too intimidating to learn. You can build the foundational coding skills you need to get started and gain confidence in your work.
